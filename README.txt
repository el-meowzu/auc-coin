African United Coin - Static Tailwind Showcase

Files:
- index.html
- about.html
- contact.html

Usage:
1. Open index.html in your browser.
2. Tailwind is loaded from the CDN, so internet access is required for styling.
3. Replace contact@africanunitedcoin.com with your real address.
4. The contact form is demo-only; connect it to your backend/Formspree/Resend to receive submissions.
